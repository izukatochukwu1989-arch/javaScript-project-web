Privacy Policy

MARC Railway Corporation PLC values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage your personal information when you use our e-ticketing website.

1. Information We Collect

i. We collect personal details such as your full name, phone number, email address, and identification details during registration.

ii. We collect travel information including departure station, destination, travel date, and booking history.

iii. Payment-related information is processed securely through trusted payment providers.

iv. Technical information such as browser type, IP address, and device information may also be collected.

2. How We Use Your Information

i. To process ticket bookings and travel reservations.

ii. To communicate important travel updates and notifications.

iii. To improve our services and customer experience.

iv. To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Ticketing Records

i. All ticket purchases are stored securely.

ii. Booking history may be used for customer support.

iii. Records help resolve disputes or complaints.

iv. Information is retained only for as long as necessary.

4. Payment Security

i. Payments are processed through secure payment gateways.

ii. MARC Railway does not store your debit or credit card PIN.

iii. Transactions are encrypted to prevent unauthorized access.

iv. Fraud prevention measures are continuously monitored.

5. Sharing of Information

i. We do not sell your personal information.

ii. Information may be shared with government agencies when required by law.

iii. Trusted service providers may receive limited information for operational purposes.

iv. Every third party must comply with strict confidentiality obligations.

6. Data Protection

i. Your information is stored using secure servers.

ii. Access is limited to authorized personnel only.

iii. Security systems are regularly updated.

iv. Appropriate safeguards protect against unauthorized access.

7. Cookies

i. Cookies help improve website functionality.

ii. Cookies remember your preferences.

iii. Analytics cookies help improve website performance.

iv. You may disable cookies through your browser settings.

8. Your Rights

i. You may request access to your personal information.

ii. You may request corrections to inaccurate information.

iii. You may request deletion where legally permissible.

iv. You may contact us regarding any privacy concerns.

9. Policy Updates

i. This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.

ii. Updates become effective immediately after publication.

iii. Significant changes will be communicated where necessary.

iv. Continued use of the website indicates acceptance of changes.

10. Contact Information

i. Contact us if you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy.

ii. Email: info@marcrailway.com

iii. Phone: +234 705 201 70328

iv. Visit our office during official working hours for assistance.
